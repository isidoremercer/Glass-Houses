# Glass-Houses

A visual novel about observation, intimacy, and the difference between watching someone & seeing them. Four romance routes with twenty unique endings.

## About

This is a copy of Glass Houses available on GitHub. To play, copy the contents of game/ to a directory with a copy of [Ren'py](https://www.renpy.org/latest.html) and open it in the launcher via renpy.exe.

### Routes

- **Charlotte Opal**: The girl who says "of course" like breathing. She cooks for five, cleans for five, and smiles for five. Ask her what she wants and watch her eyes go blank.
- **Isabella Glass**: Her best friend is an AI named Lumi. She'll tell you about the puns before she tells you why. The laptop is always open. The conversation never ends.
- **Eve Morse**: Sweet when she's here. You realize you haven't seen her in three days. When she speaks, rooms go quiet. Not because she's loud. Because she means it.
- **Amara Kismet**: Four words is a speech. She sees everything and says almost nothing. The silence isn't empty. You just have to learn how to listen.

### Features

- Four full romance routes with branching narratives 
- Twenty unique endings, including hidden endings that span across playthroughs
- Cross-route system where choices in one story unlock content in another
- Custom soundtrack (34 tracks) 
- ~200,000+ words 
- Chapter select and endings gallery with progressive hint system 

### Content Notes

Glass Houses contains themes of parental wounds, trauma, and complicated family dynamics. It is a story about love, but it has teeth. One route begins with an optional trigger warning.
